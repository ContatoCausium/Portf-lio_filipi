Privacy policy · LGPD

Your data.
In plain sight.

Last updated November 17, 2026

This is my personal portfolio. It has no sign-up, no login and nothing for sale, and it collects as little as possible. Here is, in plain words, what happens to your data when you visit the site or reach out to me through it.

01Who is responsible for your data

Filipi Wanderley de Moraes Silva, an individual based in Recife (PE), Brazil. I am the controller, under Brazil's data protection law (LGPD), of the data handled on this site, www.filipimoraes.com.

Contact for any privacy matter: filipiwms@gmail.com.

02What the site collects

  • The "Tell me what you'd like to talk about" form. It doesn't send anything to a server: when you click "Send", it opens your email app with the message ready. I only receive your name, email and message if you choose to send that email.
  • Email and WhatsApp buttons. They open your email or WhatsApp. Whatever you send there reaches me through the service you chose, along with your email address or phone number.
  • Visit analytics. I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, see which pages are opened and which sites people come from. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you.
  • Technical access data. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which receives the technical data of each request (such as IP address, browser and time) to deliver the pages and protect the site against attacks.

The site uses no advertising cookies, no Meta Pixel and no Google Analytics. Fonts and images are served by the site itself.

03What I use it for

  • Replying to people who write to me, by email or WhatsApp.
  • Understanding, in aggregate numbers, how many people visit the site and what they read, to improve it.
  • Keeping the site online and secure.

I don't sell your data, I don't use it for advertising and I don't send messages you didn't ask for.

04Who the data is shared with

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site and runs the visit analytics.
  • The service you choose to reach me: your email provider or WhatsApp (owned by Meta). Each one follows its own privacy policy.

These services may store data outside Brazil. Other than that, I don't share your data with anyone, except when required by a court or an authority with the power to request it.

05How long I keep it

  • Messages by email and WhatsApp: for as long as the conversation is relevant. If you ask, I delete them.
  • Visit numbers: kept in aggregate, without identifying anyone, for the period set by Cloudflare.

06Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you can ask me at any time for:

  • confirmation of whether I hold any data about you, and access to it;
  • correction of wrong or outdated data;
  • deletion of your data;
  • information about who it has been shared with.

Just write to filipiwms@gmail.com. I reply within 15 days. If you believe your rights were not respected, you can also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (gov.br/anpd).

07Links to other sites

The portfolio links to LinkedIn, GitHub and the sites of my projects (CauSium and Dinequora). When you open those links, each site's own privacy policy applies.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes too.
